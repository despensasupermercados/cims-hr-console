What changes from the Forms survey

Read against the 61 responses: what each question actually collected, and what it should collect.

Ship · crew · contract number
Typed. 52 spellings for ~40 hulls ("AD", "CEL - Consellation", "QS - Azamara"); 12 blank names, first names only, one row with two people. Contract count typed, 12 blank. Nothing joins to anything.
Picked, then filled. Ship from the vessel table; crew from who the TDG file has aboard that hull; contract number from the console's count (TDG's count file + contracts finished since). One audit per crew; a ship with two specialists gets two.
Date
Typed by Dexter; rows 10/11 show it drifting from the submission date.
Stamped on the day he files. A draft keeps the day it was started.
Desktop folder · OneNote · bookmarks (16 boxes)
Ticked in full on nearly every row (OneNote 61/61, bookmarks 57–61/61). Sixteen taps that record nothing, except the SOPs folder, missing on 35 rows.
Start full, tap what is missing. The record stores the gaps. One tap on most ships, and the SOPs gap becomes visible fleet-wide.
OBP with actual count
"See attachment" on 46 of 61. The finding is in a PDF nobody can query.
Accurate / Discrepancies, then the lines (part · OBP · counted). The photo or export stays as evidence beside the lines. Repair items not in OBP as a short list, not a paragraph.
Machine status (4 boxes)
Four boxes, always all ticked. It says the checks were done, not what they found.
Per machine, pass or finding. Parts past counter life is Dexter's most repeated remark (Liberty, Enchantment, Oasis, Brilliance); it gets a field of its own with the part and the percentage.
Four ratings with four comment boxes
Comment boxes answered "NA" on 15–40 rows; the useful comments sit on the low ratings.
Rating card, comment only when it matters. 3 or below asks for one sentence and will not file without it. Each card says what 5 and 3 mean, so the scale holds from ship to ship.
Overall rating
Added late; 16 of 61 rows have it. Labelled "Ship Overall Operation's Rating" on a form that rates a person.
Required, about the crew, filed on the crew. It appears on the profile beside Ray's window and the GSM review. Whether it also feeds the Score Card is Miguel's decision (below).
Reviewer feedback
The valuable part: 300–1,500 characters, always strengths, then "However", then what was agreed. Lost in a spreadsheet cell.
Kept, structured lightly (strengths · to improve · agreed with the crew) and carried forward: the next audit of that ship or crew opens with these points and asks "fixed or still open?". Two audits in a row with the same open point is a flag, not a paragraph.
Miguel's asks of Sep 2024
"Remove ERGO, add list of missing training, uniforms, coveralls, DG3 shirt, perhaps HON." Written into row 2 of the export; never added to the form.
Added: training needed (chips), uniform & PPE, HON app in use. Mono click % as an optional field so Dexter has one place to enter his field data.
Files
SharePoint links under Miguel's personal OneDrive, through Forms. Photos and PDFs, up to four per audit; the audit's evidence lives in one person's account.
An R2 bucket, keyed ship / date / audit, served through the worker behind the console login. Not GitHub: a repository is not a photo store, and 50 audits a year of phone photos would bloat it past GitHub's limits within two years.
Memory
None. Forms cannot show Dexter the last audit, the crew's history, or what Ray and the GSM said.
The briefing screen. Last audit here with its open points, the crew's earlier audits anywhere, Ray's latest window, Rolando's, the shipboard review, field intel, document lapses. Facts, no scores.

Decisions before building

One audit per crew, or per ship?

Today one form covers one visit and names one person, and breaks when there are two (Vision: "Sharene \ Karl"). Proposed: a visit is the frame, each specialist aboard gets their own evaluation, the ship-level checks (workstation, machines, tools, OBP) are filled once. Recommend: per crew within one visit.

What the overall rating does in the HR console

Phase 1: an audit card on the crew profile and the Score Card's evidence list, read as a fact. Phase 2, if wanted: the audit pre-fills Dexter's contributor window (mono click %, inventory, technical, overall) the way the shipboard review pre-fills the supervisor evaluation: pre-fill only, manual wins, never commits. Phase 2 is a bonus input, so a money PR for Miguel to merge. Phase 1 is not.

Where the files live

An R2 bucket named cims-audit-evidence already exists in the account, created 8 Jul 2026, with no record in the Brain of what uses it. Reuse it if it is this project's, or create cims-audit-files. Need: whose bucket is it?

audit.cims.work on the HR console worker

Same worker, same D1, same login, same mailer; the page is chosen by hostname and also answers at cims.work/audit. A second worker would need a service binding and a copy of the crew, ship and contract readers for one user and ~50 records a year. The domain is two lines in wrangler.toml (custom domain), created by the next deploy; no dashboard clicks. Recommend: same worker.
