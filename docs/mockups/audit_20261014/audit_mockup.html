Rules the build keeps

Works without signal

Draft saved on the phone at every tap, photos queued, filing offline, sync later. An installable PWA with a service worker, not a page.

Photos are the evidence

Take photo on every step that needs it, shrunk on the phone (1600px, ~300 KB), tagged with step and machine.

One tap when nothing is wrong

Every step starts clean. Typing is only ever about what is wrong.

Thumb sizes

Rows 48–56px, chips 40, squares 54, buttons 52, inputs 16px. Back top-left, Next bottom-right, always.

The audit is gated

No request, no audit. Resend and Extend are Dexter's; "start anyway" is nobody's.

The count is blind

Every OBP line, no on-hand figure, 0 is an answer. The crew never sees the comparison.

Colour is a finding

Red and amber appear only on what is wrong. Selection is navy. Present is quiet.

The web is the phone

Same column, a rail and the assessment beside it. One codebase, one set of screens.

Nothing here is money

Profile card only. The Score Card pre-fill is phase 2, Miguel's merge. The supervisor evaluation never.
